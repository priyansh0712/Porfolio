# Plan 08-02: Punctuality Calculator Engine & Attendance Integration

## Phase: Phase 8 - Working Schedules & Attendance Business Rules
## Plan ID: 08-02
## Objective: Build `PunctualityCalculator` engine and integrate with `AttendanceStateMachine` to automatically assign `PRESENT`, `LATE`, and `HALF_DAY` statuses to attendance records based on school working schedules.

---

### Key Deliverables:
1. `apps/schedules/calculator.py`: `PunctualityCalculator` implementing:
   - `calculate_status(school, date, check_in_time, check_out_time=None)`:
     - Check holiday exception for `school` + `date`.
     - Fetch `WorkingSchedule` for `date.weekday()`.
     - Calculate late arrival (check_in > start_time + grace_period $\implies$ `LATE`).
     - Calculate half-day (check_in > 11:00 AM OR duration < 4h $\implies$ `HALF_DAY`).
     - Calculate early departure flag.
2. `apps/attendance/services.py`:
   - Integrate `PunctualityCalculator.calculate_status` into `AttendanceStateMachine.process_scan`.
3. Unit Test Suite (`apps/schedules/tests.py`):
   - On-time check-in returns `PRESENT`.
   - Check-in after grace period returns `LATE`.
   - Late check-in after 11:00 AM returns `HALF_DAY`.
   - Holiday check-in returns `PRESENT`.
