Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about StudentERP face biometrics, privacy, & multi-tenancy.

What hardware is required for face attendance?

Zero dedicated hardware needed! StudentERP runs seamlessly on any standard laptop webcam, built-in tablet camera, or inexpensive USB webcam.

How does StudentERP protect faculty facial privacy?

We take biometric security extremely seriously. Webcam frames are immediately converted into 512-float numerical vectors in memory and raw photos are discarded instantly. No photos are ever saved on disk.

Can multiple school campuses or branches use StudentERP?

Yes! Every school institution gets a completely isolated custom subdomain (e.g. greenwood.studenterp.com) with guaranteed tenant data separation.

How are late arrivals and leaves calculated?

School administrators set custom working shift hours and grace periods (e.g. 15 minutes). The attendance engine automatically flags check-in timestamps as On-Time, Late, Half-Day, or Approved Leave.
