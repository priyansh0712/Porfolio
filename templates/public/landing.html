Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about setup, webcams, or biometrics? We have answers.

Do we need dedicated hardware or specialized cameras?

No! Standard laptop cameras or standard USB webcams connected to a desktop browser work directly out of the box with zero hardware upgrades.

How is facial data stored securely?

We follow a zero-raw-photo policy. Facial images are processed in-memory into a 512-float vector embedding and the raw frame buffer is deleted immediately. No photos are saved.

How long does onboarding take?

School self-registration takes less than 2 minutes. Once registered, your custom domain `school.ourapp.com` is active immediately.
