Who I Am

About Me

I'm a data analyst passionate about extracting meaningful insights from complex datasets. My work spans data cleaning, exploratory analysis, visualization, and storytelling — turning numbers into narratives that drive real decisions.

I work with SQL to query and transform data, Python (pandas, numpy, matplotlib, seaborn) for analysis and automation, Power BI for interactive dashboards, and Excel for business reporting.

Whether it's uncovering booking patterns in ride-sharing data or building an attendance tracking system, I believe data is only as valuable as the decisions it enables.
